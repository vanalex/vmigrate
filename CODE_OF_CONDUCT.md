# Code of conduct

vmigrate aims to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and take technical criticism constructively; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through the contact details on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/vanalex), or use GitHub's "Report content" option on
the comment or issue. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, depending on how serious the behaviour is.

This code of conduct is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
